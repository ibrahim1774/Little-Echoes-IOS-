Little Echoes lets a parent record their child's spoken answers to a daily question and keeps them as a private voice diary (memory book) of how the child sounds at every age. The app is used by parents; children do not sign in.

DEMO ACCOUNT
The demo account below already contains a child profile and recordings. To use it, tap "I already have an account" on the first screen and sign in.

WHY AN ACCOUNT IS REQUIRED
Recordings are backed up to the parent's private cloud account so they are not lost with the phone and are available on the parent's other devices. The subscription works across the same account's devices.

SUBSCRIPTIONS (all testable with a sandbox Apple ID)
New users go through a short onboarding (a few questions, then short screen recordings of the app) and then see the "Choose your plan" sheet. The plan is chosen before an account is made: no sign-up or personal information is needed to subscribe. After subscribing, the parent creates an account so recordings can be backed up, then adds a child. Signing in with the demo account skips this. Four auto-renewable plans in one subscription group:
- Audio: Basic Monthly ($4.99 a month) or Basic Yearly ($41.99 a year, with a 3-day free trial). Audio recordings only.
- Audio + Video: Pro Monthly ($9.99 a month) or Pro Yearly ($83.99 a year, with a 3-day free trial). Audio plus daily video clips.
Only the two yearly plans have a trial; the monthly plans do not.
The plan sheet has a Monthly / Yearly toggle that switches both plans.
Price, billing period and trial terms are shown on the plan sheet with links to the Terms of Use (Apple standard EULA) and Privacy Policy. A subscription (or free trial) is required to use the app; there is no free tier. If you close the plan sheet, the screen behind it has See Plans, Restore Purchases, Terms of Use and Privacy Policy (plus Sign Out and Delete account when signed in), so all of these work without subscribing. They are also in Settings. To reach the rest of the app, start either yearly free trial with your sandbox Apple ID (no charge is made in sandbox).

WHERE TO FIND FEATURES
- Home > Start Recording: today's three questions, record, review, re-record.
- Record tab: free-form recording.
- Video tab: daily video clip (Pro). Basic subscribers see an upgrade screen here.
- Memories tab: Timeline, Calendar and Growth views; play or delete any recording.
- Settings: profile, reminders, Manage subscription, Restore Purchases, Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, and Delete account (permanently deletes the account and all recordings and videos from our servers).

PERMISSIONS
Microphone (to record the child's voice) and camera (Pro video clips) are requested only when the parent starts recording. Notifications are requested on the onboarding screen where the parent picks a daily reminder time; declining only turns reminders off. The app asks for App Tracking Transparency permission at launch; it is used only for AppsFlyer attribution of our own ads, and the app works the same whether it is allowed or denied.
