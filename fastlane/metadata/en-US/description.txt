Little Echoes is a voice journal for parents. Each day, ask your child one simple question, record their answer with a single tap, and keep the way they sound at every age.

Kids grow up fast, and their voices change before you notice. The way they say "spaghetti". The songs they make up. The stories they tell at bedtime. Little Echoes keeps those moments so you can hear them again in five, ten or twenty years.

HOW IT WORKS
• Daily questions chosen for your child's age, from toddlers to 12-year-olds
• One tap to record — every answer is saved automatically
• Add your own questions, or record anything you like
• Gentle reminders on the days and at the time you choose
• A daily streak to help recording become a habit

YOUR CHILD'S MEMORY BOOK
• A timeline of every recording, organized by day
• A calendar to find any moment in seconds
• Growth view: hear their 3-year-old voice next to their 5-year-old voice
• Short video clips so you can see their face as well as hear their voice (Pro)
• Profiles for each of your children

PRIVATE AND BACKED UP
• Recordings are stored in your private account and backed up to the cloud
• Sign in on a new iPhone and your memories are still there
• Share a recording with grandparents whenever you like
• Delete any recording, or your whole account, at any time

Made for parents who want to remember the little things: first words, funny mispronunciations, favorite songs and big questions.
